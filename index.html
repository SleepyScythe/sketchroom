SleepyScytheStudios › Sketchroom

Sketch, paint and make pixel art in your browser

Pressure-aware brushes, layers, a brush editor and your own fonts. It works with a mouse, a finger or a pen.

Sign in with Google to keep your images, folders and projects in your account and open them on any device.

A quick sketch stays in this browser only.

What's in the room

Brushes
About 50 brushes, from pencils and inks to airbrush and texture. Open any brush in the editor, change it, and keep your version in My brushes. You can also import brushes you own.
Layers
Stack, hide, lock, merge and blend layers. Bring in a picture on a locked layer to trace it.
Pixel art
Start a tileset at the tile size you pick. The pencil, line, shapes and fill draw whole pixels, and you can export the result 8× bigger.
Type
Place text with about 90 free fonts, or upload a font file you are allowed to use.
Library
Images, folders and projects live in your account, so a drawing you start on an iPad is waiting on your laptop.
On your iPad
Use Share, then Add to Home Screen, and Sketchroom opens like an app.

    My brushes

    Tweak any brush in the Edit tab, then save it to My brushes.

    Import reads Sketchroom brush files, .brush and .brushset files from iPad painting apps, Photoshop .abr brushes, and PNG or JPG pictures (which become a stamp brush). Brushes from other apps come across as close copies: the tip shape carries over, but textures and some settings don't. Only import brushes you made, bought or are allowed to use. They stay private in My brushes.
